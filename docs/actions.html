Actions

Symbol buttons, menus and dialogs. Buttons are raised: hover lifts them, press sinks them. A menu floats; a dialog sits at the overlay level and covers everything. No JavaScript: menus use <details>, dialogs open as popovers or with showModal(). 

<button class="ui-button ui-button-accent"><svg>…</svg>Save</button>

<details class="ui-menu">
  <summary class="ui-button"><svg>…</svg>More</summary>
  <div class="ui-menu-list">
    <button class="ui-menu-item"><svg>…</svg>Edit</button>
  </div>
</details>

<button class="ui-button" popovertarget="d">Open</button>
<dialog class="ui-dialog" popover id="d">…</dialog>

Symbol button

An icon leads, a short label always follows. Default, accent and danger; small, medium and large; disabled goes flat.

Menu

A floating list below its button. While it's open the button stays lifted. ui-menu-end aligns it to the button's end.

Dialog

A card at the overlay level, with the card's parts. The first button opens it as a popover (no JavaScript); the second as a modal with showModal(), which also makes the page behind it inert.
