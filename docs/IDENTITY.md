# Identity

**Depth. Material. Symbol. Bring your own.**

melui is plain CSS. Neutral, so your product shows.

## Depth

The Z axis carries meaning.

- Light mode: you look from the sky down. Things cast shadows.
- Dark mode: you look into the deep. Things catch light.
- Higher means more important.
- Hover lifts. Press sinks. Selected stays up. Disabled goes flat.
- Levels: sunken, base, raised, floating, overlay, top.

## Material

Surfaces are material.

- Solid: plain. The default.
- Glass: see-through.
- Paper: fine grain.
- Ink: dark and bold.
- Make your own.

## Symbol

Symbols carry the message.

- Icons, images, materials and arrangement speak first.
- Type is limited: four sizes, two weights.
- Labels are optional. Your call.
- Icons are filled.

## Bring your own

melui brings depth, material and symbol. You bring the rest.

- Your colours.
- Your font.
- Your icons.

A theme is your CI: colours and font. Nothing else.

## Rules

- Text meets AAA contrast.
- Colour is for status and accent only.
- Plain CSS. No JavaScript.
- Small: under 30 KB, gzipped.
