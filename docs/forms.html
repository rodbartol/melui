Forms

Controls sit sunken in solid and lift to the surface while focused. Every control has a visible label. Invalid controls get a danger edge; checked ones fill with the accent.

Fields

Text input, text area and select, each in a field with a label and a hint or an error. Tab through them: each lifts while focused.

Choices

Checkboxes, radios and switches, each inside its label. Groups use a <fieldset class="ui-field"> with a legend.
