Neutral surfaces, real materials, true depth.

Melui is a small, class-based CSS toolkit for apps and websites. It stays out of sight, so your product's identity shows, not Melui's. Components are made of materials, sit at a height, and speak through symbols.

See the components Compare themes

Use it

<link rel="stylesheet" href="melui.css" />

<article class="ui-card">
  <h3 class="ui-card-title">Koi pond</h3>
  <p class="ui-card-text">Twelve fish, three lilies.</p>
</article>

Every component combines four independent properties: a material (ui-material-paper), an elevation (ui-elevation-floating), a symbol and a placement. Change one and the others stay. Theme it by overriding tokens, or try a sample theme with the switcher above.
